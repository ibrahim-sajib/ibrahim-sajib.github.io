Md Ibrahim

Associate Software Engineer
Softzino Technologies
I am currently an Associate Software Engineer at Softzino Technologies, where I have been building web applications for more than 2 years with Vue, Laravel, PHP, TypeScript, and JavaScript. With extensive expertise in backend development, particularly with Laravel, MySQL, REST API, and payment gateway integration, I can also work with other web technologies like React js, and Next js. I am keen on continuously deep-diving into new technologies to further enhance my skill set.

My research interests are broadly in AI, NLP, ML, DL and HCI and my goal is to design and build reliable AI systems that can comprehend human language's nuances, minimizing communication ambiguity by understanding context, tone, and intent and are aligned with the requirements humans have from technology. I often rely on knowledge, methods, and perspectives from multiple disciplines to address complex problems or questions that cannot be fully understood or solved within the boundaries of Computer Science.

Besides this, I was a regular competitive programmer and solved more than 1500 problems and participated in various programming contest such as ICPC Dhaka Regional. Before, joined in Softzino Technologies, I was a vice-president of Uttara University Programming Club (UUPC).
